What are Astakula Social Media Tools?

Astakula Social Media Tools are browser-based utilities developed by Ariyo Ardiwardana under Astakula to help prepare visual assets before publishing. The current set covers carousel splitting, feed-grid arrangement, and adjustable safe-zone guides.

What these tools do not do

They do not publish to social platforms, connect to your social accounts, or promise that platform UI and crop behavior will never change.

Frequently asked questions

Are uploaded images sent to Astakula?

No Astakula application backend is used by these three tools. The image workflows run in the browser.

Do I need an account?

No account is required by the current implementation.

Are safe-zone presets official platform specifications?

No. They are adjustable practical layout guides. Platform interfaces can change, so final placement should still be checked in the target app.
